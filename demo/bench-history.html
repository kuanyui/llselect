llselect benchmark history

This page runs the mass-instantiation benchmark of the benchmark page on every collected version of llselect. The native <select> runs beside them as the reference. Lower is better.

  • A round runs every version once in each scenario, and the next round runs them in reverse order.
  • Every run loads a fresh page in a frame, like a reload of the benchmark page.
  • Each number is the median of a row's successful runs. A stopped round is left out.
  • Each version runs with its own vanilla theme.
  • The entry "current" is the build in dist/, which the benchmark page loads too.

During a run, keep this tab in front and do not scroll. A tab in the background gets fewer animation frames. Scrolling adds work to the measured frames.
